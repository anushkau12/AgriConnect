How an order flows

  1. Buyer places an order
    e.g. 750 kg tomatoes, delivered to their yard.
  2. Matching engine finds farmers
    Nearest farmers with available stock are combined until the quantity is covered.
  3. A transporter is assigned
    Closest available vehicle with enough capacity for the combined load.
  4. The best route is determined
    Depot → best pickup order → buyer, minimizing total distance.
  5. Delivery is tracked
    Status moves from route planned → picked up → delivered.
